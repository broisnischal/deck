Is Deck an AI?

No. Deck is the app you run AI coding agents in. It uses the agents you already have, like Claude Code or Codex, signed in with your own accounts, and makes their work easy to follow and control.

Do I need Claude Code or another agent?

To get the most from Deck, yes: at least one coding agent CLI, such as Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Grok or OpenCode. Without one, Deck is still a fast terminal and browser.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every account gets 14 days of Deck with everything in it, counted from the day you first sign in, and no card is asked for. After that, Deck asks you to choose a plan; your chats and settings stay as they are.

Is my code sent anywhere?

Not to us. Your agents run on your Mac and talk to their own providers directly, as they would in a terminal. Deck's server only knows your account and your plan.

Does it replace my code editor?

No. Keep your editor for writing code yourself. Deck is where agents do their work, and where you watch, approve, test and review it.

Which Macs does it run on?

Any Mac with macOS 13 Ventura or later, Apple silicon or Intel. One download covers both.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes, from Settings › General › Manage Plan or your account page. You keep Deck until the end of the period you've paid for, and you won't be charged again.
