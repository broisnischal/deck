About the trial, paying, and cancelling.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every account gets 14 days of Deck with everything in it, counted from the day you first sign in, and no card is asked for. When they're up, Deck asks you to choose a plan; your chats, tabs and settings stay as they are.

How do I pay?

Click Subscribe, sign in with Google, and pay on the checkout page. Dodo Payments handles the payment and the receipt as our merchant of record, so the charge on your statement may name them.

How do I cancel?

In Deck, open Settings › General › Manage Plan, or use your account page. Your plan stays active to the end of the period you paid for, and you won't be charged again.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If Deck isn't right for you, email us within 14 days of your first payment for a full refund. The details are in the refund policy.

Do I need my own accounts for the agents?

Yes. Deck runs the agent CLIs you have installed, such as Claude Code or Codex, signed in to your own accounts with those providers. What they cost is between you and them.

How many Macs can I use it on?

Any Mac you sign in on with your account.

What happens if I'm offline?

Deck checks your plan every few hours and keeps working for up to a week without a connection.

Do you see my code or prompts?

No. Your agents run on your Mac and talk to their providers directly. Our server only knows your account and your plan. See the privacy policy.
